## 2. Function: Customer Onboarding

### 2.1 SubFunction: Customer Record Creation

#### 2.1.1 Business Rule: Customer Number Assignment Strategy on Customer Creation

##### 2.1.1.1 Purpose
A new customer record is always created with the same personal, address and contact details. The way the customer number (CUSTOMERNUMBER) is assigned depends on a control switch. If the switch is ON, the caller decides the identity of the customer by supplying the number. Otherwise the database generates the number automatically. The two original rules, one for caller-supplied and one for database-generated numbers, are consolidated here as two branches of one policy. They differ only in the source of the key.

##### 2.1.1.2 Rule Steps
1. Evaluate the customer-number control switch to choose the number assignment strategy.
   - Conditions: `LGAC-NCS` is 'ON' (caller-supplied number) or any other value (database-generated number).
   - Actions: Select the insert variant matching the switch value. Treat any value other than 'ON' as a request for automatic number generation.
   - Outcomes: The strategy for assigning the customer identifier is fixed before the row is written.
   - Relevant Paragraphs: INSERT-CUSTOMER

2. Create the customer with an explicitly supplied customer number (switch ON).
   - Conditions: `LGAC-NCS` = 'ON'.
   - Actions: Insert a new customer row. `DB2-CUSTOMERNUM-INT` is written to CUSTOMERNUMBER. The details written with it are `CA-FIRST-NAME`, `CA-LAST-NAME`, `CA-DOB`, `CA-HOUSE-NAME`, `CA-HOUSE-NUM`, `CA-POSTCODE`, `CA-PHONE-MOBILE`, `CA-PHONE-HOME` and `CA-EMAIL-ADDRESS`.
   - Outcomes: The customer is registered under the number the caller chose. This lets the caller control the identifier, for example for migration, replication or reconciliation with an external system.
   - Relevant Paragraphs: INSERT-CUSTOMER

3. Create the customer with a database-generated customer number (switch not ON).
   - Conditions: `LGAC-NCS` is not 'ON'.
   - Actions: Insert a new customer row with the same personal, address and contact fields as in step 2 (`CA-FIRST-NAME`, `CA-LAST-NAME`, `CA-DOB`, `CA-HOUSE-NAME`, `CA-HOUSE-NUM`, `CA-POSTCODE`, `CA-PHONE-MOBILE`, `CA-PHONE-HOME`, `CA-EMAIL-ADDRESS`). Omit the customer number so that the database default generates it.
   - Outcomes: The customer is registered under a system-assigned unique number. This is the standard path for new customers.
   - Relevant Paragraphs: INSERT-CUSTOMER

##### 2.1.1.3 Business Significance
The customer number is the key that links the customer to every downstream policy, claim and enquiry. If the switch is misinterpreted or wrongly set, the following can occur:
- Customers are created with unintended or colliding identifiers.
- Supplied numbers are ignored, which breaks reconciliation with external systems.
- Generated numbers are used where fixed numbers were required, which leaves records orphaned or duplicated.

Both branches must also write the same set of personal and contact attributes. If they diverge, customer data quality becomes inconsistent depending on how the customer was onboarded.
