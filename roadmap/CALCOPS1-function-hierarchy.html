CALCOPS1: Function → SubFunction → Business Rule → Step

Each step belongs to one business rule, each rule to one SubFunction and each SubFunction to one Function. A step is the smallest unit: one condition → action → outcome. Hover over any box to trace its path up and down the hierarchy.

FunctionA major capability: what the program does for the business.
SubFunctionA group of related rules: one theme of that capability.
Business RuleA named policy made up of ordered steps.
StepOne check: a condition, the action taken and the outcome (often a status code).

Worked example: "divide 10 by 0"

Function2 Controlled Arithmetic Processing
SubFunction2.3 Arithmetic Calculation Policies
Business Rule2.3.4 Division Policy
Step 2Reject zero divisor
WS-OPERAND-B = 0
OutcomeStatus '20'. Division is not attempted.
Reading top-down, the function is controlled arithmetic, the theme is calculation policies, the policy is division and the step is "reject zero divisor". Reading bottom-up, the status '20' came from step 2 of the Division Policy rule.
